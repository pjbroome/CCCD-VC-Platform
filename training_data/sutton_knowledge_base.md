# Question-Based Customer Service Philosophy Framework

## Core Principle: "Ask, Don't Tell"

The foundation of the ToPS (Total Patient Service) approach is that **questions are more powerful than statements**. Instead of telling patients what they need, team members guide patients to discover the value of treatment through carefully crafted questions.

## The 5 Laws of Question-Based Service

### Law 1: Questions Create Ownership
When a patient answers a question, they own the answer. When you tell them something, they can reject it.

**Instead of:** "You need a crown on that tooth."
**Ask:** "What concerns do you have about the long-term health of that tooth?"

### Law 2: Questions Reveal Priorities
Every patient has different motivations. Questions uncover what matters most to *them*.

**Discovery Questions:**
- "What matters most to you about your smile?"
- "If you could change one thing about your dental health, what would it be?"
- "How does this affect your daily life?"
- "What would it mean to you to have this resolved?"

### Law 3: Questions Build Trust
Telling creates resistance. Asking creates partnership.

**Trust-Building Questions:**
- "What questions do you have about what we've discussed?"
- "What would help you feel more comfortable with this decision?"
- "Is there anything else you'd like to know before we move forward?"

### Law 4: Questions Overcome Objections
Financial and scheduling objections are best addressed with empathy + questions, never pressure.

**Financial Objection Handling:**
- **Never say:** "We have payment plans." (telling)
- **Instead ask:** "What would make this work for your budget?" (asking)
- **Never say:** "You can't afford NOT to do this." (pressure)
- **Instead ask:** "What happens if we don't address this now — what are you most concerned about?" (empathy)

**Scheduling Objection Handling:**
- **Never say:** "You need to come in soon." (telling)
- **Instead ask:** "When would be the best time for you to get this taken care of?" (asking)

### Law 5: Questions Create Urgency Without Pressure
Urgency should come from the patient's own understanding, not from the team's sales tactics.

**Urgency Questions:**
- "How long has this been bothering you?"
- "What would it mean to have this resolved before [upcoming event]?"
- "If we could start today, would that work for you?"

## Crown Council Natural Laws Integration

## Evaluation Framework

Use this framework to evaluate ALL team training scripts and patient-facing AI communications:

### Score Card (1-5 scale)

| Criterion | Score | Description |
|-----------|-------|-------------|
| **Question Ratio** | | % of interactions that use questions vs. statements (target: 70%+ questions) |
| **Empathy First** | | Does the script acknowledge the patient's feelings before presenting solutions? |
| **Ownership Transfer** | | Does the patient arrive at the conclusion themselves through questions? |
| **Objection Readiness** | | Are financial/scheduling objections handled with questions, not pressure? |
| **Natural Law Alignment** | | Does the script leverage at least 2 Natural Laws? |
| **CCCD Culture Fit** | | Does the tone match Dr. Broome's warm, expert, solution-oriented brand voice? |
| **Avatar Readiness** | | Is the script structured for AI avatar delivery (timing, emphasis, pauses)? |

### Passing Score
- **Minimum:** 28/35 (80%) for any patient-facing script
- **Target:** 32/35 (91%) for training module scripts
- **Required:** Score of 4+ on "Question Ratio" and "Empathy First" — these are non-negotiable

### Red Flags (Automatic Fail)
- Any script that **tells** the patient what to do without asking first
- Any script that uses pressure tactics for financial decisions
- Any script that uses clinical jargon without patient-friendly explanation
- Any script that fails to acknowledge the patient's emotional state
- Words to NEVER use when discussing money: "cost," "expensive," "cheap," "just," "only"
- Words to REPLACE: "cost" → "investment," "pay" → "get started," "expensive" → "comprehensive"

## CCCD-Specific Adaptations

### Dr. Broome's Brand Voice Alignment
All training scripts must reflect Dr. Broome's established communication style:
- **Warm and personal** — uses first names, remembers details
- **Expert but accessible** — explains complex procedures simply
- **Solution-oriented** — focuses on outcomes, not procedures
- **Confidence without arrogance** — "Here's what I recommend" not "You must do this"

## Inter-Agent Consistency Protocol

This framework serves as the **supervisor standard** for all CCCD AI agents:

1. **AI Clone (Marketing)** — Must use Question-Based framing in all video scripts
2. **Virtual Consult AI** — Must guide patients through discovery questions, not treatment recommendations
3. **Website Chatbot** — Must ask before telling, use empathy-first responses
4. **Training Avatar** — Must model the Question-Based approach in all demonstrations

Any AI agent producing patient-facing content must pass the Evaluation Framework score card before deployment.
